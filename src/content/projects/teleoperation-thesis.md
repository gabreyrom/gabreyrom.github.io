## Problem

Hands-on engineering labs typically require students to be physically present with the equipment, which limits access for remote or distance learners.

## Contribution

Undergraduate thesis for the dual Mechatronics and Industrial Engineering degrees at ITAM, titled "Design and Implementation of a Heterogeneous Teleoperation System for Remote Engineering Education."

## Approach

The thesis designed and implemented a teleoperation system allowing engineering lab equipment to be operated remotely, aimed at remote engineering education.

## Supported results

Recognized with the Best Thesis Award at the XXVI Premio de Investigación ExITAM, 2021 — ITAM's award for best undergraduate research.

## Limitations

The linked repository holds the code developed for the thesis; a full technical write-up beyond the thesis title and award is not published on this page.
